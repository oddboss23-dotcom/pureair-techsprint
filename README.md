## 📌 Overview

PUREAIR is an AI-powered atmospheric intelligence system designed to monitor, analyze, and predict air quality risks across Delhi NCT.

The platform integrates real-time AQI monitoring, spatial analytics, predictive modeling, and health risk simulation into a unified decision-support dashboard.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🌍 Real-Time AQI Monitoring  
- 🗺️ Spatial Risk Heatmap (Sector-Level Analysis)  
- 📊 Toxicity Rankings Across Municipal Sectors  
- 📈 Predictive Exposure Forecast (24h / 48h / 72h)  
- 🏥 Health Impact Simulation (Digital Twin Model)  
- 🏛️ GRAP Governance Integration  
- 🚨 Executive Emergency Action Matrix  
- 🤖 AI-Powered Atmospheric Modeling  

---

## 🧠 Intelligence Layers

PUREAIR operates across multiple intelligence layers:

1. Government Air Quality Guidelines (GRAP)
2. Active Enforcement Status
3. Spatial Monitoring Node
4. Atmospheric Analytics Engine
5. Predictive Exposure Modeling
6. Executive Decision Orchestrator
